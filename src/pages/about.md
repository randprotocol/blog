This is the engineering blog of [Rand Protocol](https://randprotocol.org/), a
post-quantum, fully shielded chain with a zkVM. The team writes here about how
the protocol is designed and built, and what we learn along the way.

## Elsewhere

- [randprotocol.org](https://randprotocol.org/): the protocol, in overview.
- [Papers](https://randprotocol.org/papers): the protocol and implementation papers.
- [Documentation](https://randprotocol.org/docs): for developers and node operators.
- [Explorer](https://randscan.org/): the chain, as it runs.
- [GitHub](https://github.com/randprotocol): the source.

## Corrections

The posts live in [github.com/randprotocol/blog](https://github.com/randprotocol/blog).
Every post links to its source, so a correction can be sent as a pull request.
